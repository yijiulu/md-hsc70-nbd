Reading the View

  • Subdomain IB sits upper-left and IIB upper-right; closure toward the central cleft decreases domain angle δ (top track).
  • Trp90 (IB) and Arg261 (IIB) span opposite walls of the nucleotide cleft (middle track, Woo et al. 2009 metric).
  • Enabling "Linker crevice residues" highlights the 4B9Q-mapped IA/IIA linker-binding slot; inter-wall centroid distance is shown in the bottom track.
  • The grey CA trace marks crystal structure 1KAX as a stationary spatial reference.

Interpretation Notes

This viewer compares one replica per nucleotide state. Slow domain fluctuations occur over tens of nanoseconds, so statistical inference requires multi-replica aggregation (see summary CSVs). Block averaging filters fast local thermal noise to emphasise collective subdomain motion; the shaded initial window marks the equilibration cut excluded from quantitative summaries.
